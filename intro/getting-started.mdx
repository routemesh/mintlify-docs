RouteMesh gives you **access to all RPC providers in one**. We charge on a **per-request basis**, with no hidden fees or minimum commitments. You get **15+ providers** and **1000+ chains** with **auto-retries and failovers** built in.

Say goodbye to compute units, failing requests, juggling multiple providers, slow requests, and overage charges. One bill, one URL, transparent pricing.

## **Why RouteMesh**

- **One bill, many chains** — Simplify your subscriptions. No lock-in plans; pay only for what you use.
- **Transparent pricing** — We work with 15+ providers so you get a single, clear price per request. Economy keys start around $3 per million requests. Performance keys around $6 per million for the fastest routes.
- **High reliability** — If a request fails, we retry across multiple providers so you get a response. Redundancy is baked in.
- **Data quality** — We continuously validate that nodes return correct, up-to-date data. See [Data quality](/intro/data-quality) for how it works.

We built RouteMesh for crypto natives who’ve been price-gouged, hit by unresponsive providers, or burned by failing archive calls. So you can focus on building for the next billion users.

Ready to go? Head to [Quick Start](/intro/quick-start) to sign up, get your API key, top up, and make your first request.
