The provider **Dashboard** is your home view in the [RouteMesh app](https://routeme.sh/app/provider). It summarizes key metrics and links to deeper insights.

## **Summary metrics**

- **Total requests** — Requests routed to your provider in the selected time range
- **Market share** — Your share of routed traffic (by chain/strategy as configured)
- **Active routes** — Number of routes (chain + method + region + strategy) where you are currently eligible
- **Revenue** — Revenue from routed traffic in the period; **all-time revenue** is also shown

## **Market share by chain**

The dashboard shows your market share (and request volume) by chain and routing strategy (e.g. economy vs performance). Use this to see where you are winning traffic and where there is room to grow.
