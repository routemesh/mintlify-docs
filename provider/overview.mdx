RouteMesh connects **node providers** (RPC infrastructure) with **consumers** (dApps, wallets, and developers) through a single routing layer. As a provider, you expose your nodes to RouteMesh; we route consumer traffic to you based on pricing, latency, and strategy—and you earn revenue when your routes are selected.

## **Become a provider**

Want to join RouteMesh as a node provider? **You don’t need any special software.** We only need your **RPC endpoint URL(s)** and **pricing**—we handle routing, billing, and the consumer interface.

Reach out and we’ll walk you through onboarding.

## **Who is the provider portal for?**

- Blockchain RPC / node infrastructure providers (e.g. Alchemy, QuickNode, Ankr, and others)
- Teams that operate nodes and want to reach RouteMesh’s consumer base
- Organizations that already have a provider agreement with RouteMesh

## **What you get in the portal**

- **Dashboard** — Requests, market share, active routes, and revenue at a glance
- **Plans, methods, nodes** — Configure pricing (plans), supported methods, and node endpoints
- **Inspect** — Drill into request-level behavior, latency, and quality checks (replay/lag)
- **Market share** — See your share and competition by chain, method, and strategy
- **Data quality** — We run replay and lag checks to keep data correct and fresh; see [Data quality](/provider/data-quality) for how they work and what to do if you see failures
