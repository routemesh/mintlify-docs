## **What happens after a failure**

Nodes that fail [replay](/provider/replay-checks) or [lag](/provider/lag-checks) checks are not permanently banned. They are typically moved out of production for a period and must pass checks again before they can receive traffic. Once they consistently pass, they are reintroduced. This gives you a chance to fix issues without losing access permanently.

## **Where to see your results**

In the provider portal, open [Inspect](/provider/inspect). You can filter by time range and view metrics related to replay and lag checks for your nodes. Use this to spot problems and confirm that fixes are working.

If you have questions about your own results, reach out to [k@routeme.sh](mailto:k@routeme.sh).
