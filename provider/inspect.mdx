title: "Inspect"
---

**Inspect** (under **Insights** in the provider portal) lets you drill into request-level behavior and latency for your routes. Use it to debug issues, compare performance across chains/methods, and tune your nodes and plans.

## **What you can do in Inspect**

- **Filter** by chain, method, region, strategy, and time range.
- **View latency** — e.g. distributions (box plots) and trends over time for your routes.
- **Compare** your latency and success rates to others (where available) to see how competitive you are.
- **Identify** slow or failing routes and fix node or plan configuration.

## **How to use it**

1. Go to [Provider → Inspect](https://routeme.sh/app/provider/inspect).
2. Select the chain(s), method(s), and time range you care about.
3. Review latency and volume charts and tables.
4. Use the data to adjust [Nodes](/provider/nodes) or [Plans](/provider/plans) as needed.

Inspect complements the high-level [Dashboard](/provider/dashboard) and the strategy-focused [Market share](/provider/market-share) view.
