title: "Plans"
---

**Plans** define how you price your routes on RouteMesh. In the provider portal, **Plans** (under **Management**) let you create and manage plans that apply to specific chains, methods, and regions.

## **What is a plan?**

A plan ties together:

- **Pricing** — e.g. cost per million requests (or similar unit)
- **Scope** — Which chains, methods, and regions the plan applies to
- **Strategy** — Whether the plan is for economy or performance routing (where applicable)

Consumers’ traffic is routed based on their strategy and your (and others’) plan pricing and availability.

## **Managing plans in the portal**

1. Go to [Provider portal → Plans](https://routeme.sh/app/provider/portal/plans).
2. Create a new plan or edit an existing one.
3. Set the rate (e.g. per million requests), chain(s), method(s), and region as needed.
4. Save; your routes will update and become eligible for routing according to the router’s logic.

## **Tips**

- Competitive pricing and broad coverage (chains/methods) tend to increase market share.
- Use [Market share](/provider/market-share) and [Inspect](/provider/inspect) to see how plan changes affect traffic and latency.
